How it works

Built for the shop, not the boardroom.

The work orders, parts and maintenance your crew already deals with, from the phone in their pocket.

1

Maintenance that writes its own work orders

"Oil change every 5,000 miles or 6 months, whichever comes first." Set it once for every F-150, or every mower you own, and FleetOptima keeps count for each one.

  • Home shows what needs you today: open jobs, what's due, what's coming up
  • When it's due, the work order makes itself
  • Finish the job and the count starts over from the real reading
2

Work orders from the phone in your pocket

Operators report a problem in seconds, and a machine that's down can be marked urgent so it jumps the line. Mechanics start it, pull parts, add notes and close it out. Every step is on the record: who did it and when.

  • Scan a part's barcode with the camera
  • Read an odometer, an hour meter or a serial number off a photo
  • Search any list, and filter jobs: open, in progress, done
3

Parts and costs, down to the quart

Pull parts onto a job and they leave the shelf, priced oldest stock first. So you finally see what each truck and mower costs to run.

  • Spend by month, by machine and by part
  • Cost per mile or per hour for every asset
  • Every pull and restock on the record, so the shelf count stays honest
4

See where the money goes

One page that answers "what is this fleet costing me?" Which machines eat the most, what that works out to per hour or per mile, and which parts still need a price. Work you send out counts too: the tire shop, the body shop, the dealer, and who you paid the most.

  • The last month, 3 months, 6 months or any range
  • The machines costing the most, ranked
  • Only the people you choose can see the money

Questions

Good to know

Machine or small tool?

A machine has a VIN or a meter: trucks, trailers, mowers, chippers, generators, pressure washers. $8 a month each. A small tool has neither: chainsaws, pole saws, blowers, trimmers, and the backups for them. $1 a month each, so you can track all of it. People, parts and work orders are free.

Do I need to install anything?

No. FleetOptima runs in the browser on any phone or computer. On a phone you can add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.

What happens when the trial ends?

Pick a plan and keep going. If you don't, your data stays put and FleetOptima goes read-only: you can see everything, just not add or change anything, until you pick a plan.

Is this a finished product?

Yes. Everything on this page works today. What comes next is shaped by what customers ask for, so tell us what you need.
