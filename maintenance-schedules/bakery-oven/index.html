Commercial bakery oven maintenance schedule

For deck, convection and rack ovens. In a bakery, OSHA wants the oven's safety devices checked twice a month by a trained employee, so most of this sheet is built around that routine.

Required by code? OSHA 1910.263 covers bakery equipment. It requires oven safety devices to be inspected at least twice a month by a specially appointed, trained bakery employee, and at least once a year by the oven maker's representatives. It also requires gas burners to shut off the fuel if the pilot or flame fails. Code items apply where your city, state or insurer has adopted them. Requirements vary by jurisdiction, so check local code.

What changes these intervals

How many hours a day it bakes, your water hardness if it makes steam, and flour and lint in the air, which pack into burner areas and air intakes.
