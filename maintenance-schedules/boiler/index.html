For small commercial hot water and steam boilers. Steam boilers need a lot more attention than hot water ones, and this is the sheet where state law matters most.

Required by code? Many states require periodic boiler inspections by a state or insurance inspector, and some set how often you test the low-water cutoff (one state requires it daily). Which boilers are covered varies, and some small hot-water heating boilers are exempt. Code items apply where your city, state or insurer has adopted them. Requirements vary by jurisdiction, so check local code.

What changes these intervals

Steam versus hot water (steam needs far more blowdown and cutoff testing), water quality, and your state's boiler law.
