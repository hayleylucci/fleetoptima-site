Chainsaw maintenance schedule

For gas chainsaws on a working crew. The daily checks are mostly safety: the chain brake, the throttle lockout, and a chain that doesn't creep at idle.

Download the PDF

Typical intervals from manufacturer manuals and published service schedules. Your machine's manual comes first.

Every day

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Look the whole saw over for damage and leaksDaily
Test throttle, throttle lockout and stop switchDaily
Test the chain brakeDaily
Check the chain is getting oilDailyPoint it at a stump and rev; you should see a line of oil
Check chain sharpness and tensionDaily and every refuel
Chain doesn't move at idleDailyAdjust idle if it creeps
Clean the air intake and cylinder finsAs needed

Weekly

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Check the sprocketWeeklyReplace when worn
Check the bar, file off burrsWeeklyFlip the bar for even wear
Sharpen the chainWeekly at minimumDaily in dirty wood

Monthly and longer

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Clean the air filterMonthlyReplace if damaged
Replace the spark plugEvery 100 hours
Decarbon the exhaust portAbout every 150 hoursShop job
Chain brake serviced by a dealerEvery 3 months full-time, 6 part-time, 12 occasional
New saws: re-tighten cylinder base screwsAfter the first 10 to 20 hoursBigger professional saws

What changes these intervals

Hours per day (a full-time tree crew versus occasional use), dust, and the wood. Dirty, sandy or very resinous wood dulls chains and clogs filters fast.
