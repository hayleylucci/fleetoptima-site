Commercial mixer maintenance schedule

For planetary and spiral mixers in bakeries and kitchens. The bowl guard interlock is what keeps hands out of a running mixer, so check it every day, not just at the yearly service.

Required by code? In bakeries, OSHA 1910.263 requires a mixer's belts, gears and other moving parts to be fully enclosed, and a handy manual switch that keeps the mixer from being started while it's cleaned or serviced. Keep those covers on and that switch working. Code items apply where your city, state or insurer has adopted them. Requirements vary by jurisdiction, so check local code.

What changes these intervals

How often it runs and how heavy the batches are. Makers note that a mixer may need service more or less often depending on use.
