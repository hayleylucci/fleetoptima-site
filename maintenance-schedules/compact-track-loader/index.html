Compact track loader maintenance schedule

For compact track loaders. Most of this matches a skid steer, plus the undercarriage: packed mud in the rollers and the wrong track tension are what wear tracks out early. The makers really disagree on engine oil, so check yours.

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Track tensionEvery 50 to 100 hoursRecheck after mud or clay; sag spec per your manual

Air filterCheck about every 50 hours, or per the indicator
Fuel filtersEvery 500 to 1,000 hours or yearly

Hydraulic fluidEvery 1,000 hours or yearly
CoolantEvery 2 yearsSome makers say 1,500 hours

What changes these intervals

Dust, extreme heat or cold, and fuel from an outside storage tank. Mud and clay mean more cleaning and more tension checks.
