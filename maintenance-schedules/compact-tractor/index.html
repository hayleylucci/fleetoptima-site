Compact tractor maintenance schedule

For compact utility tractors in the 25 to 60 hp range. One fluid usually runs the transmission and the hydraulics both, so its filter matters more than people think.

Yearly

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Engine oil and filter, fuel filterYearly, even if hours are low
Wheel bolt torqueYearly
Hoses and clampsYearly
CoolantEvery 2 yearsLonger with some makers' own coolant

What changes these intervals

Dust and heat (filters and oil), long idling and short run times (oil), and wet conditions (grease far more often).
