Powder coating cure oven maintenance schedule

For batch and conveyor cure ovens, gas or electric. Powder cures on part temperature, not oven air temperature, so a heavy part can come out under-cured from an oven that reads right on the dial.

Required by code? NFPA 86 requires safety interlocks to be function-tested at least yearly, interlock set points verified at least yearly, safety shutoff valves leak-tested at least yearly, and that testing written down. It also requires training for the people who run and maintain the oven. Code items apply where your city, state or insurer has adopted them. Requirements vary by jurisdiction, so check local code.

What changes these intervals

Hours of use, how heavy the parts are, and dust building up on burners and fans. Heavy parts take longer to come up to temperature, and a changed load needs a new profile.
