Diesel pickup (towing) maintenance schedule

For 3/4- and 1-ton diesel pickups that pull equipment trailers: Ford Super Duty Power Stroke, Ram Cummins and GM Duramax. All three makers put a towing truck on the severe schedule, and all three count idle time against oil life.

What changes these intervals

Towing near the weight limit, idle and PTO hours, dust, and biodiesel. A truck that tows equipment every day belongs on the severe schedule with any of the three makers.
