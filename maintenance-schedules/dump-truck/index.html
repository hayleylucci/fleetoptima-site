Dump truck maintenance schedule

For medium-duty dump trucks, the F-550 to F-750, Isuzu NPR, Ram 5500 and International MV class. Dump work is severe duty almost by definition: idling, low speeds, heavy loads and dust. So use the severe schedule, and track engine hours as well as miles.

Typical intervals from manufacturer manuals and published service schedules. Your truck's manual and its oil-life monitor come first. Your dump body and hoist maker's manual covers the body.

Every day

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Check hoist oil levelDailyPark level and cycle the hoist empty a few times first
Hoist oil looks cleanDailyMilky, dark or thin oil gets changed right away
PTO off before drivingEvery time
Tailgate unlocked to dump, locked afterEvery dump
Body prop in place before anyone works under a raised bodyEvery time
Air brakes: drain the air tanksDailyAir brake trucks only, or check the auto drains work

Weekly and monthly

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Inspect hydraulic hosesWeeklyReplace worn ones right away
Check nuts, bolts, cotter pins and control linkageWeekly
Drain the fuel water separatorMonthlyRight away if the water-in-fuel light comes on
Grease hoist pivots, hinge pins, tailgate hinges and latchesAbout every 150 dumps or 2 monthsWhichever comes first

Engine and chassis (severe duty)

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Engine oil and filterPer your engine, on the severe scheduleOften around 250 to 350 engine hours or 5,000 to 12,000 miles; use hours if the truck idles a lot
Fuel filtersEvery other oil change (severe)Per your manual
Air filterCheck the restriction gauge each oil change
Coolant strengthAbout every 15,000 miles or 600 hours
Lube king pins and the front endEvery service

Yearly

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Change the hoist hydraulic oilYearlySooner with heavy use or dust
Clean the hoist reservoir breatherEach hoist oil change
Adjust tailgate latchesAs needed

Required by code? If the truck is a commercial motor vehicle (most dump trucks are), federal or state rules may require a yearly inspection, written maintenance records and daily inspection reports. See the daily truck inspection checklist for who that applies to. Requirements vary by state, so check with yours.

What changes these intervals

Dump cycles per day, dust, idle and PTO hours, and running at or near the weight limit.
