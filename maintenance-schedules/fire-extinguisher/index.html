For portable, wall-mounted extinguishers. The monthly check is quick and anybody can do it. The yearly service and the longer dates are for a licensed service company.

Required by code? Monthly inspections and yearly maintenance are required by NFPA 10 where it's adopted, and by OSHA 1910.157 for extinguishers provided for employees, which also requires keeping the yearly service record. Extinguishers exposed to weather, vehicles or tampering may need checking more often than monthly. Code items apply where your city, state or insurer has adopted them. Requirements vary by jurisdiction, so check local code.

What changes these intervals

The type (CO2 and water units have a 5-year hydro test, most dry chemical 12) and where it hangs. One in a truck, outdoors or near a busy door needs checking more often.
