Forklift maintenance schedule

For propane, gas, diesel and electric warehouse forklifts. OSHA requires a check before the truck goes to work, and a truck that fails it gets parked until it's fixed, not run to the end of the shift.

Required by code? OSHA 1910.178 requires every powered industrial truck to be examined at least daily before it's put in service, and after each shift when trucks run around the clock. A truck that isn't safe has to come out of service until it's repaired. OSHA also requires each operator's performance to be evaluated at least every 3 years. Code items apply where your city, state or insurer has adopted them. Requirements vary by jurisdiction, so check local code.

What changes these intervals

Hours, number of shifts, dust and load. A truck running two or three shifts hits 250 hours fast, and round-the-clock trucks get checked every shift, not once a day.
