HVAC unit maintenance schedule

For rooftop (packaged) units and split systems in a shop or small commercial building. Filters are the monthly job; everything else is a spring check before cooling season and a fall check before heating season.

Typical intervals from manufacturer manuals and published service schedules. Your machine's manual comes first.

Monthly

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Check air filters, replace when dirtyMonthlyMost get replaced every 1 to 3 months; dusty sites more often
Condensate drain pan and line clearMonthly in cooling seasonClogged drains are the classic water-on-the-floor call

Quarterly

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Blower belt tension and wearQuarterlyBelt-drive units only
Clear debris from around the outdoor coilQuarterlyLeaves, cottonwood, trash

TaskHow oftenNotes✓

Clean the indoor (evaporator) coilYearly
Check refrigerant chargeYearlyTechnician
Tighten electrical connections, check motor ampsYearly
Check thermostat and controlsYearly

Fall (heating check)

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Gas connections, gas pressure, burner combustionYearlyGas heat only; technician
Inspect the heat exchangerYearlyCracks can leak carbon monoxide
Clean and adjust the blower, lubricate moving partsYearlyWhere the unit has lube points

What changes these intervals

Dust, pollen and nearby kitchen exhaust (filters and coils clog faster), plus how many hours it runs. A rooftop unit on a dusty site may need filters every few weeks.
