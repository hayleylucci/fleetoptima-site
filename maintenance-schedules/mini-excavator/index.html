Mini excavator maintenance schedule

For compact (mini) excavators. Grease and track tension are the daily habits. Work in water or mud and the swing bearing wants grease far more often.

Download the PDF

CoolantEvery 2 years
Hydraulic hosesReplace every 6 yearsPer at least one major maker

What changes these intervals

Dust, working in water or mud (swing bearing grease), and hammer work, which is hard on the whole hydraulic system.
