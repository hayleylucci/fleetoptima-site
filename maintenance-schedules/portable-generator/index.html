Portable generator maintenance schedule

For gas portable generators, the 3 to 10 kW job-site and inverter kind. The first oil change comes fast on a new one, and older carbureted models want oil about twice as often as newer inverter models.

Typical intervals from manufacturer manuals and published service schedules. Your machine's manual comes first.

TaskHow oftenNotes✓

Replace the spark plugYearly or about 300 hours
Valve clearanceEvery 100 to 1,000 hoursVaries a lot by model; usually a shop job

Yearly and longer

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Oil, plug and filters, even if hours are lowYearlyA generator that barely runs still needs these
Check the fuel lineEvery 2 years
Replace the fuel lineAbout every 3 years

What changes these intervals

Heavy load and hot weather (oil), dust (air filter), and engine type: older carbureted generators want oil about twice as often as newer inverters.
