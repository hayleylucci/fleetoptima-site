Skid steer maintenance schedule

For skid steer loaders. The 10-hour list is the daily one: grease every pivot, check the fluids, and make sure the seat bar locks out the controls.

Download the PDF

Typical intervals from manufacturer manuals and published service schedules. Your machine's manual comes first.

TaskHow oftenNotes✓

Every 10 hours (daily)

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Check engine oil and coolant10 hoursCoolant with the engine cold
Check the air filter indicator10 hoursService the filter when it says to
Clean the radiator10 hours
Drain water from the fuel filter10 hours
Grease lift arms, cylinders, attachment plate and pivots10 hours
Seat belt, seat bar and interlocks10 hoursWith the bar up, lift and tilt shouldn't work
Tires, lights, horn, decals10 hours

By hours

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Hydraulic fluid level, hoses, chaincase level, park brake, wheel nutsEvery 50 hours
Spark arrester, battery cablesEvery 100 hours
Engine oil and filterEvery 150 to 500 hours or yearlySmall machines are on the short end; every 100 hours in dusty, hot or cold work
Fuel filter, drive beltsEvery 250 hours or yearly
Hydraulic filter and reservoir breatherEvery 500 hours or yearly
Hydraulic fluid and chaincase fluidEvery 1,000 hours or yearly
CoolantEvery 2 years

What changes these intervals

Dust, extreme heat or cold, and fuel from an outside storage tank (water in the fuel). The machine's size matters too: small frames change oil more often.
