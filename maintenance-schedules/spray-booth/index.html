Powder and spray booth maintenance schedule

For dry-filter paint booths and powder booths with cartridge recovery. The gauge tells you when the filters are done, so read it every day and change filters by the reading, not the calendar.

TaskHow oftenNotes✓

Nitrocellulose coatingsChange filters at least daily

Required by code? OSHA 1910.107 requires spray areas to be kept as free of combustible residue as practical (cleaned daily if needed), filter media to be inspected and replaced, and conductive objects near electrostatic powder guns to be grounded. Where your fire code adopts NFPA 33, the spray application standard, it adds booth rules of its own. Code items apply where your city, state or insurer has adopted them. Requirements vary by jurisdiction, so check local code.

What changes these intervals

How much you spray, what you spray (nitrocellulose coatings call for daily filter changes), and the weather, since intake filters clog fast in smog, fog or frost.
