Standby generator maintenance schedule

For home and light-commercial standby generators that sit ready and run during outages. Most of the work is yearly, but long outages pile on run hours fast, so the oil change can come due in the middle of one.

Required by code? Generators that are legally required emergency power (like in health care or life-safety systems) fall under NFPA 110, which adds weekly inspections, monthly 30-minute load runs, monthly transfer switch tests and written logs. A typical optional standby unit at a home or small shop usually just follows the manufacturer's schedule. Code items apply where your city, state or insurer has adopted them. Requirements vary by jurisdiction, so check local code.

What changes these intervals

Run hours from long outages, very hot or cold weather, and dust. A week-long outage can put a whole year's worth of hours on the engine.
