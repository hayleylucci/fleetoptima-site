String trimmer and leaf blower maintenance schedule

For 2-stroke string trimmers and backpack or handheld blowers. On a full-time crew these rack up hours long before the calendar catches up, so go by whichever comes first.

Download the PDF

Typical intervals from manufacturer manuals and published service schedules. Your machine's manual comes first.

Every day

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Clean the air filterDaily
Clean the cooling fins and intakeDailyOverheating kills these engines
Check for fuel leaksDaily and every refuel
Check the starter ropeDaily
Tighten loose screws and boltsDaily

Every 3 months or 90 hours

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Replace the air filter3 months or 90 hoursSome makers say only when it loses power; replacing on schedule is cheaper than a rebuild
Check the fuel filter3 months or 90 hours
Check the fuel cap gasket3 months or 90 hours
Clean or check the spark plug3 months or 90 hours
Check the spark arrester screen3 months or 90 hoursReplace if clogged
Decarbon the exhaust port3 months or 90 hoursShop job

Longer

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Replace the spark plugEvery 100 to 270 hoursDepends on the maker
Replace the fuel filterYearly
Replace the fuel cap gasketYearly
Replace the starter rope if wornYearly

What changes these intervals

Dust (filters and plug fouling) and full-day commercial use. Those 90-hour and 3-month limits are maximums, so heavy use means sooner.
