Stump grinder maintenance schedule

For walk-behind and self-propelled stump grinders. Rocks and dirt in the stump chew up teeth and pockets, so the daily wheel check matters more than anything else on this sheet.

Typical intervals from manufacturer manuals and published service schedules. Your machine's manual comes first. Grease points and intervals vary by model, so follow your manual for those.

Every day or 10 hours

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Walk around itDaily
Check engine oil and fuelDaily
Check hydraulic oil levelDailyHydraulic models
Check the air cleanerDaily
Inspect the cutter wheel and teethDailyTurn it by hand. Flip or replace teeth once they're duller than your fingertip; outer teeth wear fastest
Clean off grease and oil buildupDaily

What changes these intervals

Dirt and rocks in the stump (teeth and pockets) and dust (air filter).
