Vehicle lift maintenance schedule

For two-post and four-post automotive lifts. The daily check takes a minute and is the one that keeps a truck off somebody's head.

Typical intervals from manufacturer manuals and published service schedules. Your machine's manual comes first.

Yearly and beyond

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Inspection by a qualified lift inspectorAt least yearlyANSI/ALI standard; see note below
Change hydraulic fluidEvery 2 yearsUse the hydraulic oil your manual specifies, not transmission fluid

Required by code? The yearly inspection by a qualified lift inspector comes from the ANSI/ALI ALOIM standard. It's a voluntary standard, not a federal law by itself, but OSHA can cite recognized standards like it, and insurers often ask for it. Code items apply where your city, state or insurer has adopted them. Requirements vary by jurisdiction, so check local code.

What changes these intervals

How many times a day it cycles, and moisture. Lifts in wash bays or open-air bays rust cables and pulleys much faster.
