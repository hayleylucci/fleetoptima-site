Walk-behind mower maintenance schedule

For commercial walk-behind mowers with hydro drive. Most of the work happens every 25 hours, which on a busy crew is about once a week.

What changes these intervals

The engine brand (oil intervals run anywhere from 40 to 100 hours), dust, and which hydraulic oil you use.
