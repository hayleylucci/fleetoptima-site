Walk-in cooler and freezer maintenance schedule

For walk-in coolers and freezers. Most failures start small: a torn door gasket, a dirty condenser coil, or a clogged drain line in the freezer.

Download the PDF

Typical intervals from manufacturer manuals and published service schedules. Your machine's manual comes first.

Daily

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Check and log the box temperatureDailyFood service: your health department may require the log
Keep floors clean and dryDailyNever hose down the inside of the box

Weekly to monthly

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Door gaskets: tears and sealWeekly to monthlyClose the door on a dollar bill; it should hold
Door closes and latches on its ownMonthlyFreezers: check the door frame heater
Anti-slip floor stripsMonthly

Quarterly (condensing unit)

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Clear dirt and debris from the condenser coilQuarterlyMonthly if it's near a kitchen or on a dusty roof
Look for oil stains and leaksQuarterly
Check the sight glassQuarterlyBubbles or moisture mean call a tech
Listen for noise and vibration, check wiringQuarterly

Twice a year

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Clean the evaporator coil inside the boxEvery 6 months
Clear the drain pan, drain line and trapEvery 6 monthsSooner if it clogs
Check fans and defrost controlsEvery 6 monthsFreezers: check the drain line heater too
Full condensing unit serviceEvery 6 monthsTechnician: pressures, controls, safeties

What changes these intervals

Where the condensing unit sits (kitchen grease or a dusty roof clogs the coil fast), how often the door opens, and freezer versus cooler (freezers fight ice and defrost problems).
