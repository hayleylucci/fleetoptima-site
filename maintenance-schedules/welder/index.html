Welder and plasma cutter maintenance schedule

For MIG, TIG and stick machines and shop plasma cutters. Most of it is quick: clean the tip and nozzle every day, check cables every few months, and blow the dust out of the machine before it builds up.

Download the PDF

Typical intervals from manufacturer manuals and published service schedules. Your machine's manual comes first.

Daily

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
MIG: clean the nozzle, check the contact tipDailyReplace either one when worn
Coil the gun cable looselyDaily
Shut off the shielding gas when doneDaily
Plasma: check inlet air or gas pressureDaily
Plasma: inspect consumables, torch and torch leadDailyReplace consumables as needed
Plasma: coolant level and conditionDailyLiquid-cooled systems

Weekly to monthly

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Plasma: leak test the gas linesWeekly
Tighten the work clamp lead and gun cable connectionsMonthly
Plasma: check ground and table-to-workpiece connectionsMonthlyClean, bare metal-to-metal contact
Plasma: clean inside the power supplyMonthlyQualified person; check contactors and relays

Every 3 months

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Inspect weld cables, replace cracked onesEvery 3 months
Clean and tighten weld terminalsEvery 3 months
Inspect gas hoses and fittingsEvery 3 months
Blow out the MIG gun linerEvery 3 monthsFiltered compressed air; replace the liner when worn
Replace unreadable labelsEvery 3 months

Every 6 months

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
Blow out or vacuum inside the welderEvery 6 monthsPower disconnected; monthly in heavy use
Clean the wire drive rollsEvery 6 months
Plasma: coolant system serviceEvery 6 monthsLiquid-cooled systems; qualified person

What changes these intervals

Arc time, grinding dust in the air, and how the cables get treated. Heavy use or a dusty shop means cleaning the inside monthly instead of twice a year.
