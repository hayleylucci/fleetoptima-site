Wood chipper maintenance schedule

For towable disc and drum brush chippers. Knives are the whole game. Sharp knives pull wood in and save the engine, belts and bearings.

Download the PDF

Chipper makers publish less of their schedules than most, so treat this sheet as the minimum and check your manual, especially for bearing grease, where over-greasing can do damage too.

What changes these intervals

The wood. Dirty, sandy or frozen wood dulls knives fast, and dull knives put load on everything else. After that, hours per day.
