Work truck maintenance schedule

For crew pickups and service trucks. Almost every work truck belongs on the severe schedule: towing, hauling, idling at job sites and dirt roads all count. Track engine hours as well as miles, because idling wears the engine without adding miles.

What changes these intervals

Towing and hauling, idle time, and dust or dirt roads. Any of those puts the truck on the severe schedule.
