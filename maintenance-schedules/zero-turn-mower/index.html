Zero-turn mower maintenance schedule

For commercial zero-turn mowers. Everything runs on engine hours, so the hour meter is your calendar. The first oil change on a new mower comes fast.

Typical intervals from manufacturer manuals and published service schedules. Your machine's manual comes first.

Break-in (new mowers)

TaskHow oftenNotes✓
First engine oil change5 to 8 hoursDepends on the engine
First hydraulic filter and fluid change100 to 250 hoursVaries by model; check your manual

What changes these intervals

Dust is the big one: it eats air filters and oil. After that, the engine brand and which oil and filters you use, since some makers stretch intervals only with their own synthetic.
