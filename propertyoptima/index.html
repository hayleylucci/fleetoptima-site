How it works

Built for landlords, not leasing offices.

The leaks, the locks and the filters you already deal with, without the phone tag.

1

Tenants report it from their phone

No more texts at midnight with half the story. Tenants pick their place, say what's wrong, and mark it urgent if it can't wait. If it's a real emergency, the app tells them to call 911 first.

  • Tenants only ever see their own place and their own requests
  • They see when it's started and when it's done, so they stop asking
  • Invite them with a link or a QR code. They pick their own password
2

Work it, on the record

You or your maintenance person start it, add notes, log the plumber or the HVAC guy and what they charged, and close it out. Every step says who did it and when, and it can't be edited after the fact.

  • Urgent requests jump the line, and Home counts them first
  • Email alerts for new requests, or only the urgent ones
  • A record you can point to when a deposit or a dispute comes up
3

Maintenance that reminds you

"Change the HVAC filter every 3 months. Test the smoke detectors every 6." Set it once for every property, and PropertyOptima keeps the dates for each one.

  • Home shows what needs you: open requests, what's due, what's coming up
  • When it's due, the request makes itself
  • Mark it done and the clock starts over
4

See what each property costs

One page that answers "what is this place costing me?" Every plumber, locksmith and roofer you paid, by month and by property, and the jobs still waiting on a receipt.

  • The last month, 3 months, 6 months or any range
  • The properties costing the most, ranked
  • Only the people you choose can see the money

Questions

Good to know

What counts as a property?

Each place you rent out: a house, each unit of a duplex, a mobile home. Tenants, your maintenance person and the requests themselves are free.

Do my tenants need to install anything?

No. PropertyOptima runs in the browser on any phone or computer. You send them a link or show them a QR code, they pick a password, and they're in. On a phone they can add it to the home screen and it opens like an app.

What happens when the trial ends?

Pick a plan and keep going. If you don't, your data stays put and PropertyOptima goes read-only: you can see everything, just not add or change anything, until you pick a plan.

Why do I see the FleetOptima name in places?

PropertyOptima is built on FleetOptima, the same app crews and shops use to keep their equipment maintained, set up for rentals. Same company, same people. You'll see the FleetOptima name in a few spots while PropertyOptima gets its own home.

Is this a finished product?

Everything on this page works today, and landlords can use it right now. The app lives at beta.fleetoptima.org while PropertyOptima is new, and what comes next is shaped by what landlords ask for, so tell us what you need.
