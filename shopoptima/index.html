How it works

Built for the shop floor, not the front office.

The breakdowns, filters and scheduled checks you already deal with, from the phone in your pocket.

1

Your crew reports it from the floor

Pick the machine, say what's wrong, done. A machine that's down can be marked urgent so it jumps the line. No yelling across the shop, no sticky note that falls off the booth.

  • Invite your crew with a link or a QR code. They pick their own password
  • Each person only sees what you let them see
  • Nothing to install. It runs in the browser on any phone
2

Work it, on the record

Your maintenance person starts the job, pulls the parts, adds a note and closes it out. Every step says who did it and when, and that history can't be edited after the fact.

  • Scan a part's barcode with the camera
  • Pulled parts leave the shelf, priced oldest stock first
  • Log outside work too: the oven tech, the compressor service, and what they charged
3

Maintenance that writes its own work orders

"Check the oven's temperature profile every 3 months. Service the forklift every 250 hours." Set it once, and ShopOptima keeps count for each machine.

  • By hours, by the calendar, or by miles for the shop truck
  • When it's due, the work order makes itself
  • Finish the job and the count starts over from the real reading
  • Home shows what needs you today: open jobs, what's due, what's coming up
4

See what each machine costs

One page that answers "what is this shop costing me?" Which machines eat the most, what that works out to per hour, and which parts still need a price. Work you send out counts too.

  • The last month, 3 months, 6 months or any range
  • The machines costing the most, ranked
  • Only the people you choose can see the money

Questions

Good to know

Machine or small tool?

A small tool is the handheld stuff: grinders, drills, sanders, and the backups for them. $1 a month each, so you can track all of it. Everything else is a machine: ovens, booths, compressors, blast cabinets, lifts, welders, the forklift and the shop truck, anything with a meter or a service schedule. $8 a month each. People, parts and work orders are free.

Isn't this for vehicles?

No. ShopOptima tracks anything that runs on hours or the calendar, and miles too if you have a shop truck. Most of what's in a shop never leaves the floor, and that's exactly what it's set up for.

Do I need to install anything?

No. ShopOptima runs in the browser on any phone or computer. On a phone you can add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.

What happens when the trial ends?

Pick a plan and keep going. If you don't, your data stays put and the app goes read-only: you can see everything, just not add or change anything, until you pick a plan.

Why do I see the FleetOptima name in places?

ShopOptima is built on FleetOptima, the same app crews use to keep their equipment maintained, set up for shops. Same company, same people. You'll see the FleetOptima name in a few spots while ShopOptima gets its own home.

Is this a finished product?

Everything on this page works today, and shops can use it right now. The app lives at beta.fleetoptima.org while it's new, and what comes next is shaped by what shops ask for, so tell us what you need.
